## What the UI reads that changed (all in core, `session/`)
1. **Optional questions.** After the four mandatory answers the planner may ask up to 2 more, ONE at a time, never in an urgent lane: tools/items ("Which tool, machine or item was involved?" or "Was it the printer?" with Yes/No), who was hurt, "Did anyone see it happen?". They render through the generic `question.asks` loop in `Cards.kt` with 2 replies, not the 5 severity buttons: **None** / **No one** (`QuickReply.NONE`, recorded as an explicit none) and **Not sure** (`QuickReply.SKIP`, records nothing). Design note: the dock's own "Skip" text action is `vm::goCheck`, it abandons ALL remaining questions and goes to Check and send; for optional questions that action is better labelled "Check and send".
2. **Spoken answers.** While a single optional question is open, a short spoken answer ("Meera and Ravi", "no one", "pata nahi") answers it without the first pass (`ReportSession.answerOpenQuestion`). Nothing to render; it just means the mic stays the main control.
3. **Chips from the second look.** `SessionUi.suggestions` can now contain chips whose `source == Source.LLM` (reason text starts "I may have missed it: you said ..."), arriving 2 to 3 seconds AFTER the turn (the app calls `ReportSession.probeMissing()` after the turn, under the model lock). Please let a chip appear without re-laying out the whole report, and keep Accept and Dismiss as they are.
4. **Wording.** `Labels.fieldLabel(EQUIPMENT_ASSET)` is now "Tools / items" (spoken "Tools or items"); nobody should see the word "Equipment" in the UI. Voice commands accept tool, tools, item, items.
5. **Hindi.** The Home "Hindi (experimental)" switch is `AppViewModel.setHindiLane`; with the Hindi lane on and the Supertonic model present, replies are spoken in Hindi (`HindiPhrases`), the screen text stays English by design. Please keep the Hindi switch visible but labelled experimental.
